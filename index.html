Who's at the bench?

Twenty-four real specimen patches, pathology nuclei and dermoscopy lesions, each already roughly segmented by an automatic model. Correct the boundary back toward the truth with the brush or lasso. Speed and accuracy both score.

How scoring works
Match
Overlap (Dice) between your corrected mask and the real ground truth.
Speed
A bonus that fades out after about 90 seconds on a patch. Careful beats frantic, but don't stall.
Streak
Consecutive clean matches (≥85%) build a streak. A rough one resets it.
Before you begin

What you'll be looking at

Two quick examples of the raw photos in this bench, no markings, just what the tissue/lesion actually looks like. In the real task, each photo already has a rough boundary drawn on it by an automatic model. Your job is to correct that boundary with the brush or lasso tools, not draw one from scratch.

Take your time to look at the texture and edges here. The real patches are the same size and quality as what you see now. When a patch loads for real, look for where the drawn boundary doesn't match the true edge of the nucleus or lesion, and correct just that.
